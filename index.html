Today's Animal Stars

Learn something new about these amazing creatures!

🦊
Forest

Red Fox

Foxes are super clever! They can hear a mouse under 3 feet of snow and jump on it exactly.

🌍 Found worldwide ⚖️ 3–11 kg 📏 45–90 cm
🐬
Ocean

Bottlenose Dolphin

Dolphins are so smart they have names for each other! They call friends by a unique whistle.

🌊 All oceans ⚖️ 150–650 kg 📏 2–4 m
🦋
Insect

Monarch Butterfly

Monarchs travel up to 4,500 km every year without a map — just using the sun as their compass!

🌻 North America ⚖️ 0.5 g 📏 10 cm wingspan
🐘
Wild

African Elephant

Elephants never forget — they can remember the faces of hundreds of friends for decades!

🌍 Africa ⚖️ up to 6,000 kg 📏 3–4 m tall
